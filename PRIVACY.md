# Privacy Policy — BGA Terraforming Mars Advisor

Effective: 2026-06-11

This extension does **not collect, transmit, sell, or share any user data**.

- All processing happens locally in your browser on boardgamearena.com pages.
- The only stored data is your extension preferences (enabled, badge/tooltip
  visibility, tier filter), kept in Chrome's `chrome.storage.sync`, which
  Chrome may sync between your own signed-in browsers. Nothing else is stored.
- The extension makes **no network requests** of its own. It only reads the
  Terraforming Mars game page you already have open in order to display
  rating badges on it.
- No analytics, no telemetry, no third-party services, no accounts.

Questions: open an issue at
https://github.com/mralnis/terraforming-mars-bga-advisor/issues
